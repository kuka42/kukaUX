EDEN - Release 24

Eight o'clock Departure, Everon, Nineteen eighty-nine.
A text adventure set on Everon during the 1989 evacuation.
About 1-2 hours. Three endings.
Story by kuka.

Play on the KNS tablet:
- put eden.z5 in Documents/My Games/ArmaReforger/profile/KNS/zmachine
- type "zmachine eden" in the terminal

Also runs in any Z-machine interpreter (e.g. Frotz, Gargoyle, Parchment).

Compiled with Inform 6.45, library 6.12.2.
